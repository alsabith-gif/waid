HERITAGE AND PROCESS

The Goodyear Welt Process

A true Goodyear welt is the gold standard of shoe construction. Using a ribbon of leather stitched directly to the upper and the insole, we create a cavity filled with natural cork that molds to your unique footprint over time.

This complex process requires over 200 individual manual steps but creates a water-resistant, ultra-supportive foundation that can be dismantled and resoled again and again.
